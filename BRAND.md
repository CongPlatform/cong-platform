\# CONG Brand Policy

This policy applies to the name, logos, mascot, and visual identity of the CONG project.

\## Relationship with the software license

The source code of CONG is licensed separately under the MIT License.

The MIT License does not grant permission to use the CONG name, logos, mascot, visual identity, or other brand elements as the identity of another product, organization, service, or project.

No trademark license or other brand license is granted by the software license.

\## Protected brand elements

For the purposes of this policy, CONG brand elements include, among others:

\- the name "CONG" and "Construtor Operacional para ONGs";

\- CONG logos and logo variations;

\- the Cong mascot and its visual variations;

\- official visual identity elements specifically associated with CONG.

The presence of these assets in a public repository does not place them under the MIT License.

\## Permitted uses

You may use the CONG name and brand elements when reasonably necessary to:

\- refer factually to the CONG project;

\- identify an authentic contribution to CONG;

\- discuss, review, teach about, or document the project;

\- display screenshots of CONG for informational or educational purposes;

\- develop and test contributions intended for submission to the official CONG project.

Such use must not imply sponsorship, endorsement, partnership, or official status when none exists.

\## Forks and derivative projects

The MIT License allows forks and derivative works of the source code.

However, a fork or derivative project that is distributed, publicly deployed, or operated independently from the official CONG project must not present itself as the official CONG service.

Independent projects should use their own name, logo, mascot, and visual identity.

References such as "based on CONG", "fork of CONG", or "compatible with CONG" are permitted when they are accurate and do not create confusion about the project's origin or official status.

\## Uses requiring permission

Unless expressly authorized by the relevant rights holders, you may not use CONG brand elements:

\- as the primary branding of another application, service, organization, or product;

\- in a way that suggests an unofficial project is maintained, approved, sponsored, or endorsed by CONG;

\- for merchandise or commercial promotional material;

\- in domains, social media accounts, or identities that could reasonably be mistaken for official CONG channels;

\- in altered forms presented as official CONG branding.

\## Contributions

Brand assets may be modified when the modification is part of a contribution proposed to the official CONG project.

Acceptance of a contribution does not grant the contributor ownership of the CONG brand or authorization to use it outside the project.

\## No registration claim

This document does not claim that any CONG name, logo, or other brand element is a registered trademark.

It defines the permissions granted by the project regarding use of its brand assets and reserves rights not expressly granted here.

\## Questions and permissions

Questions about uses not covered by this policy should be discussed with the CONG project maintainers before using the brand assets.
